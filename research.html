RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

Start with the business.
End with the thesis.

I approach equity research by combining business understanding, financial analysis, valuation and risk assessment rather than relying on a single metric or narrative.

01

Understand the Business

Understand how the company makes money, where growth comes from and what could strengthen or weaken its competitive position.

02

Follow the Numbers

Use financial statements and operating metrics to test growth, profitability, cash generation and capital efficiency.

03

Value the Expectations

Understand the growth, margins and returns that the current valuation appears to assume.

04

Challenge the Thesis

Identify the assumptions that could invalidate the thesis and assess the potential downside.

RESEARCH PROCESS

From research question to investment view.

01

Define the Question

Start with a clear research question. What makes the company interesting, what is changing and what needs to be understood?

02

Research the Industry

Map industry structure, growth drivers, competitive dynamics, regulation and cyclicality.

03

Understand the Business

Break down the business model, revenue streams, customers, products and competitive advantages.

04

Analyze the Financials

Examine historical growth, profitability, cash generation, capital efficiency and leverage.

05

Build the Valuation

Translate operating assumptions into projected financials and valuation.

06

Stress-test the Thesis

Challenge assumptions through downside scenarios, sensitivity analysis and risk assessment.

07

Form the Investment View

Bring the evidence together into a concise thesis covering opportunity, drivers, valuation and risks.

VALUATION

Value the business,
not just the stock price.

01 — Assumptions

Define revenue growth, margins, reinvestment, working capital and cost of capital.

02 — Forecasts

Translate operating assumptions into projected financial statements.

03 — Valuation

Apply appropriate intrinsic or relative valuation methods.

04 — Sensitivity

Test how changes in growth, margins and valuation assumptions affect estimated value.

RISK & SCENARIO ANALYSIS

Know what can break the thesis.

Business Risk

Competitive position, customer behaviour, product economics or execution.

Financial Risk

Leverage, liquidity, capital requirements, cash generation and financial flexibility.

Industry & Macro Risk

Demand, interest rates, regulation, economic conditions and industry structure.

Valuation Risk

The possibility that current expectations leave insufficient room for weaker performance.

AI-ASSISTED RESEARCH

Use AI to accelerate research, not replace judgement.

AI can reduce the time required to discover, structure and analyse information. My approach is to use it as a research assistant while keeping source verification, financial reasoning and investment judgement at the centre.
