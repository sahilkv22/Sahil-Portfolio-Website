Business Overview

Understanding how the company creates value.

What the business does

Bajaj Finance will be analysed as a diversified lending business, with the research focused on how it acquires customers, deploys capital, manages credit risk, and generates returns from its lending portfolio.

The final report will replace this illustrative description with verified information from company filings, annual reports, investor presentations, and other reliable sources.

What investors should understand

  • How the lending portfolio is constructed
  • Where growth is coming from
  • How risk is managed across products
  • How efficiently capital is deployed
  • How growth translates into shareholder returns

Risk & Asset Quality

Growth is valuable only when risk remains controlled.

This section is intentionally central to the Bajaj Finance analysis because lending growth must be evaluated alongside borrower quality, delinquencies, provisions, and credit costs.

Underwriting

Evaluate borrower selection, underwriting discipline, risk segmentation, and policy evolution.

Asset Quality

Analyse delinquency trends, GNPA, NNPA, recoveries, provisioning, and portfolio composition.

Credit Cost

Determine whether changes in credit costs are consistent with portfolio growth and the underlying risk environment.

Credit-Risk Perspective

A key analytical question will be whether incremental growth is being achieved with acceptable deterioration in borrower quality and risk-adjusted returns.

Key Risks

What could invalidate the investment case?

01

Credit Cycle

A deterioration in borrower quality could increase delinquencies, provisions, and credit costs.

02

Funding & Liquidity

Changes in funding conditions or liquidity availability could affect lending economics.

03

Regulation

Regulatory changes can influence capital requirements, provisioning, product economics, or underwriting.

04

Competitive Pressure

Banks, NBFCs, fintechs, and other lenders can pressure pricing, customer acquisition, or market share.

05

Growth-Risk Imbalance

Aggressive growth without adequate underwriting discipline can weaken long-term risk-adjusted returns.
