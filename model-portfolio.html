RISK FRAMEWORK

What could go wrong?

The objective is not to eliminate risk, but to understand where the portfolio can fail and size positions accordingly.

01

Business Risk

The underlying business may fail to deliver expected revenue, earnings or cash-flow growth.

02

Valuation Risk

A high-quality company can still produce poor returns when purchased at an excessive valuation.

03

Concentration Risk

Excessive exposure to one company, sector or theme can amplify portfolio losses.

04

Macro Risk

Interest rates, inflation, regulation, economic cycles and broader market conditions can affect performance.

THESIS INVALIDATION

When should an investment be reconsidered?

01 Structural deterioration in the underlying business.

02 Material deterioration in earnings or cash flows.

03 Management or capital-allocation concerns.

04 Valuation becoming disconnected from fundamentals.
